# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Newest first.

## 0.1.0 (not released)

First build. It compiles against Stardew Valley 1.6.15 and SMAPI 4.5.2 and passes its tests, but has not been played yet.

- Flashlight tool and Dry Cell battery, sold at Robin's Carpenter's Shop, with a Dry Cell recipe.
- A forward beam in the four facing directions, with a faint glow around the farmer.
- Batteries drain while the flashlight is on and are taken from the backpack automatically.
- The beam flickers on the last charge and a message appears; it switches off when the farmer goes to bed.
